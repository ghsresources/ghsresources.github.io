The GHS Survival Guide

So, it's your first day at Glendora High School. More specifically, your first day at high school, period. The moment step foot on this campus, you will immediately notice THOUSANDS of students and staff members in the area known as the center of campus. You feel lost. You think to yourself, "I don't feel like I belong here." Don't worry. That moment happens to A LOT of freshmen on their first day at GHS. The purpose of this guide is to help you to ease your first day feelings that you may inevitably have, and offer you advice that will best allow you to make the most of your time at GHS.

First, and Foremost...

BE YOURSELF! Glendora High School is not made up of a single ideology, mindset, or passion. The GHS population consists of almost every possible kind of person you could ever imagine. Too many people believe that they must participate in X club or sport because they want to "fit in". Remember, if everyone was the same, then GHS would be pretty boring.

Clubs/Athletics

There are dozens of clubs at GHS that you can join. Examples of clubs include STEMsters, D&D club, Lifesavers, and Youth Government. During Club Rush (which happens in September and January), you will have the opportunity to explore and join clubs. You can also create a club by completing the necessary forms, found in Room 215.

If you want to join a sport, you will need to complete the registration form online for each sport.
